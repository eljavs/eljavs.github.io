Unjustified

In development · 4-person team

You wake up trapped in a death game. You meet the other contestants, build relationships with them, then face them in the games. Every contestant plays by the same rules: no special privileges, no scripted roles. Each one remembers how you treated them and decides for themselves whether to trust you, protect you, or turn on you. You are but one participant, the same as everybody else, and you’ll have to social engineer your way to the top.

  • Psychological Horror
  • Story Rich
  • Choices Matter
  • Social Deduction
