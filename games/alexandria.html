Alexandria

In development · Solo project

A first-person parkour platformer crossed with an incremental game. Run timed courses through a drained, colorless city, shoot targets, earn currency and buy upgrades that unlock new movement, and hire clones that replay your routes for passive income. Color floods back into the world as you explore it.
